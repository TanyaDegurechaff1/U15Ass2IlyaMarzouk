Coffee, Cake and Cats

Whiskers Cafe is a cat cafe where you can have a drink and some cake while spending time with our rescue cats. Some of our cats are looking for a new home too!

Book a Table

What we offer

Coffee & Cake

We sell hot drinks, cold drinks and homemade cakes, all served while you sit with the cats.

Cat Time

Every table booking gets time in the cat room. Just relax and let the cats come and say hello.

Adoption

A lot of our cats are rescues and some are looking for a forever home. Ask a member of staff if you want to know more.

Opening Times

Tuesday to Sunday, 10am till 5pm. We are closed on Mondays so the cats can have a rest day.

We would recommend booking before you come, especially on weekends, because we can only let a certain number of people in the cat room at once.
